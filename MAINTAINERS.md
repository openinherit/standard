## Extension Maintainers

Extension maintainers are listed in each extension's `extension.json` manifest
and mapped in the [CODEOWNERS](.github/CODEOWNERS) file.

As jurisdiction partners are onboarded, their details are added here.

## Decision Process

- **Bug fixes and docs:** merged by any maintainer after CI passes.
- **Schema changes:** require a [proposal](proposals/0000-proposal-template.md), review period, and approval from the core maintainer.
- **New extensions:** require a jurisdiction partner and follow the [extension guide](docs/implement/extension-guide.md).
- **Breaking changes:** follow the rules in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) — only permitted at major version boundaries with two independent implementations demonstrating the need.

## Review Expectations

- Pull requests are typically reviewed within **3 working days**.
- Schema change proposals receive an initial response within **5 working days**.
- If you haven't heard back, comment on your PR or email hello@openinherit.org.
