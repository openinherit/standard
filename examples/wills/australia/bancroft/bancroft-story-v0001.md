# Mutual Will and Testament of Thomas James Bancroft and Sarah Anne Bancroft

**Jurisdiction:** New South Wales, Australia (Succession Act 2006 (NSW))

## What this will tests

This will exercises the following INHERIT capabilities:

- **`mutualWillAgreement`** — binding contract between spouses not to revoke without written notice; constructive trust arising on first death
- **Blended family kinships** — `step_sibling` kinship types between Jack/Emily (Thomas's children) and Oliver (Sarah's child), `step_parent` kinships
- **Superannuation nomination** — Australian extension: AusSuper binding death benefit nomination to Oliver, passes outside the estate
- **Aboriginal art with cultural significance** — specific bequests with `culturalSignificance` flag and disposal restrictions (repatriation to traditional owners)
- **`familyProvisionClaims`** — acknowledged potential claim under Succession Act 2006 (NSW) Part 3.2; explicit statement of adequate provision for Oliver
- **`connections`** — business partner relationships (Bancroft Engineering Pty Ltd)
- **Life interest / survivor takes all** — everything to surviving spouse on first death
- **Equalisation payment** — cash payment to Emily to compensate for business passing to Jack
- **`asset-categories`** — real property, business (company shares), superannuation, collectibles/art, bank accounts
- **`kinship`** — parent-child biological (Thomas→Jack, Thomas→Emily, Sarah→Oliver), spousal (Thomas↔Sarah), step-parent-child
- **`administrationPhases`** — debts → funeral → specific bequests → equalisation → residue
- **`constructionClauses`** — Succession Act 2006 (NSW) construction rules, Interpretation Act 1987 (NSW)
- **`conditionType: "restriction"`** — prohibition on revocation without written notice (mutual will contract)
- **`nonprobateTransfers`** — superannuation (binding death benefit nomination outside estate)
- **Charitable bequest with conditions** — art collection to gallery with repatriation condition for culturally significant pieces

# PART A — MUTUAL WILL AGREEMENT

---

**THIS AGREEMENT AND MUTUAL WILL** is made on the **20th day of March 2024** between:

**(1) THOMAS JAMES BANCROFT** of 42 Darling Point Road, Darling Point, New South Wales 2027 (hereinafter "Thomas"); and

**(2) SARAH ANNE BANCROFT** (nee Williams) of the same address (hereinafter "Sarah"),

(hereinafter together "the Testators" and each "a Testator").

---

## RECITALS

A. Thomas and Sarah are husband and wife, having married on 8 February 2014.

B. Thomas was previously married to **HELEN BANCROFT** (nee Murray), now deceased, by whom he has two children: **JACK THOMAS BANCROFT** (born 12 June 1996, aged 28) and **EMILY GRACE BANCROFT** (born 3 September 1999, aged 25).

C. Sarah was previously married to **DAVID WILLIAMS**, from whom she was divorced on 14 September 2012, by whom she has one child: **OLIVER JAMES WILLIAMS** (born 19 April 2002, aged 22).

D. Thomas and Sarah have no children together.

E. Thomas and Sarah wish to make mutual Wills on the terms set out below, and to enter into a binding agreement that neither of them shall revoke his or her Will without giving written notice to the other.

---

## CLAUSE 1 — MUTUAL WILL AGREEMENT

1.1 In consideration of each party making a Will in the form set out in Parts B and C below, Thomas and Sarah each agree and covenant with the other as follows:

   (a) Neither Thomas nor Sarah shall revoke, alter, or amend his or her Will (as set out below) without first giving the other not less than sixty (60) days' written notice of the intention to do so;

   (b) Upon receipt of such notice, the other party shall be at liberty to revoke, alter, or amend his or her own Will;

   (c) If either Thomas or Sarah revokes, alters, or amends his or her Will without giving the required notice, the other party's Will shall remain in force and a constructive trust shall be imposed on the estate of the party in breach, such that the beneficiaries named in the non-breaching party's Will shall have the same rights as if the breaching party's Will had not been revoked, altered, or amended;

   (d) Upon the death of the first to die ("the First Testator"), the survivor ("the Surviving Testator") shall be bound by the terms of this Agreement and shall not revoke, alter, or amend his or her Will (as set out below) save with the unanimous written consent of all adult beneficiaries named in the Will of the First Testator; and

   (e) This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of New South Wales and shall be enforceable as a contract at law and in equity.

1.2 Thomas and Sarah each acknowledge that they have received independent legal advice as to the effect and consequences of this mutual Will agreement, and each enters into it freely and voluntarily.

# PART B — WILL OF THOMAS JAMES BANCROFT

---

## CLAUSE 2 — REVOCATION

2.1 I, **THOMAS JAMES BANCROFT**, hereby revoke all former Wills, Codicils and Testamentary Dispositions heretofore made by me and declare this to be my last Will, subject to the Mutual Will Agreement in Part A above.

---

## CLAUSE 3 — APPOINTMENT OF EXECUTOR

3.1 I appoint **RICHARD LEUNG** of Leung & Partners, Level 12, 60 Martin Place, Sydney, New South Wales 2000, as the sole Executor and Trustee of this my Will (hereinafter "my Executor").

3.2 If Richard Leung shall predecease me or shall be unwilling or unable to act, I appoint my son **JACK THOMAS BANCROFT** as substitute Executor and Trustee.

3.3 My Executor shall have all the powers conferred upon executors and trustees by the Trustee Act 1925 (NSW) and the Succession Act 2006 (NSW), and by law.

---

## CLAUSE 4 — PAYMENT OF DEBTS AND EXPENSES

4.1 I direct my Executor to pay all my just debts, funeral and testamentary expenses, and all duties and taxes payable by reason of my death from my estate.

---

## CLAUSE 5 — FUNERAL WISHES

5.1 I wish to be cremated, and my ashes scattered in Sydney Harbour at the location where Sarah and I were married on the harbour cruise vessel.

5.2 These wishes are precatory and not binding on my Executor.

---

## CLAUSE 6 — ON FIRST DEATH — EVERYTHING TO SURVIVOR

6.1 If my wife **SARAH ANNE BANCROFT** shall survive me by twenty-eight (28) days, I give, devise and bequeath the whole of my estate, both real and personal, of whatever kind and wheresoever situate, to Sarah absolutely.

6.2 If Sarah shall not survive me by twenty-eight days, or if we die in circumstances rendering it uncertain which of us survived the other, then my estate shall be distributed in accordance with Clause 7 below as if Sarah had predeceased me.

---

## CLAUSE 7 — ON SECOND DEATH (OR IF SARAH PREDECEASES ME)

If the provisions of Clause 6 do not take effect, or upon the subsequent death of Sarah (who shall be bound by the mutual will agreement to distribute in accordance with these terms), I direct as follows:

### (a) Darling Point Residence

7.1 I give, devise and bequeath my freehold property at 42 Darling Point Road, Darling Point, New South Wales 2027 (Lot 12, DP 654321) to my children **JACK THOMAS BANCROFT** and **EMILY GRACE BANCROFT** in equal shares as tenants in common.

7.2 If either Jack or Emily shall predecease the Surviving Testator (or, if there is no Surviving Testator, shall predecease me) leaving issue, the share of such deceased child shall pass to his or her issue in equal shares per stirpes. If either shall predecease without issue, the whole shall pass to the survivor of Jack and Emily.

### (b) Bancroft Engineering Pty Ltd

7.3 I give and bequeath the whole of my one hundred (100) ordinary shares in **BANCROFT ENGINEERING PTY LTD** (ACN 123 456 789), being the entire issued share capital of the company, to my son **JACK THOMAS BANCROFT** absolutely.

7.4 In recognition of the fact that the business has been valued at approximately **AUD 1,200,000** and that Emily does not receive any interest in the company, I direct my Executor to pay to my daughter **EMILY GRACE BANCROFT** the sum of **FIVE HUNDRED THOUSAND AUSTRALIAN DOLLARS (AUD 500,000)** (the "Equalisation Payment") from the residuary estate within twelve (12) months of the date of death of the Surviving Testator (or, if there is no Surviving Testator, within twelve months of my death).

7.5 If the residuary estate is insufficient to meet the Equalisation Payment in full, I direct that Jack shall be personally liable for the shortfall, and he shall pay the outstanding balance to Emily within a further twelve (12) months.

### (c) Aboriginal Art Collection — Bunjil Gallery with Cultural Repatriation

7.6 I give and bequeath the whole of my collection of Aboriginal art, comprising approximately forty-five (45) works and valued at approximately **AUD 180,000**, to the **BUNJIL PLACE GALLERY** (operated by the City of Casey, ABN 38 853 608 090), Narre Warren, Victoria, subject to the following conditions:

   (a) The Gallery shall accept the collection and exhibit it for the benefit of the public;

   (b) Eight (8) works in the collection originating from the Arnhem Land region, specifically those by artists **Djambu Barra Barra**, **John Mawurndjul**, and **Lena Yarinkura**, which are of particular cultural significance to the Kuninjku and Rembarrnga peoples, shall be offered by the Gallery, within twelve (12) months of receipt, for repatriation to their respective traditional owner communities through the relevant Aboriginal art centres (Maningrida Arts and Culture or such successor body as may exist);

   (c) If the relevant traditional owner communities or art centres decline to accept any of the eight culturally significant works, those works shall be retained by the Gallery and exhibited with full attribution and cultural context;

   (d) The remaining thirty-seven (37) works shall be the absolute property of the Gallery, to be exhibited, conserved, loaned, or dealt with as the Gallery sees fit; and

   (e) If the Bunjil Place Gallery is unwilling or unable to accept the collection, my Executor shall donate the entire collection to the **NATIONAL GALLERY OF AUSTRALIA** in Canberra, subject to the same repatriation condition in paragraph (b) above.

7.7 I express the wish that whichever institution receives the collection shall consult with Aboriginal elders and traditional owners regarding the appropriate display and interpretation of the works. This wish is precatory and not legally binding.

### (d) Joint ANZ Savings Account

7.8 I record that the savings account held jointly by me and Sarah at Australia and New Zealand Banking Group Limited (ANZ), BSB 012-345, Account No. 6789 0123, containing approximately **AUD 200,000**, is held as joint tenants. Upon the death of the first of us to die, the whole of the said account shall pass to the survivor by right of survivorship and does not form part of the estate of the deceased. Upon the death of the survivor, the balance of the said account (or such account as may have replaced it) shall form part of the estate of the survivor and shall be distributed in accordance with this Will.

---

## CLAUSE 8 — RESIDUARY ESTATE (THOMAS)

8.1 Subject to the payment of my debts, funeral and testamentary expenses, the Equalisation Payment, and the specific devises and bequests above, I give, devise and bequeath all the rest and residue of my estate to my three stepchildren and children — **JACK THOMAS BANCROFT**, **EMILY GRACE BANCROFT**, and **OLIVER JAMES WILLIAMS** — in equal shares absolutely.

8.2 I include Oliver in the residuary estate in recognition of his place in our family and of my affection for him as my stepson.

---

## CLAUSE 9 — FAMILY PROVISION ACKNOWLEDGEMENT (THOMAS)

9.1 Sarah and I acknowledge that **OLIVER JAMES WILLIAMS**, as a person who was, at the date of my death, a member of the household of which I was a member, and as the child of my spouse, may be an eligible person within the meaning of Part 3.2 of the Succession Act 2006 (NSW) and entitled to make a family provision claim against my estate.

9.2 We have given careful consideration to the needs of Oliver and believe that the provision made for him in this Will (his entitlement to a one-third share of the residuary estate, together with Sarah's superannuation entitlement described in Part D below) constitutes adequate provision for his proper maintenance, education, and advancement in life.

9.3 We record that, as at the date of this Will, Oliver is aged 22 years, is a full-time student at the University of New South Wales studying mechanical engineering, and is financially dependent upon Sarah and me. We have taken this dependency into account in making provision for him.

# PART C — WILL OF SARAH ANNE BANCROFT

---

## CLAUSE 11 — APPOINTMENT OF EXECUTOR

11.1 I appoint **RICHARD LEUNG** of Leung & Partners, Level 12, 60 Martin Place, Sydney, New South Wales 2000, as the sole Executor and Trustee of this my Will.

11.2 If Richard Leung shall predecease me or shall be unwilling or unable to act, I appoint my son **OLIVER JAMES WILLIAMS** as substitute Executor and Trustee.

---

## CLAUSE 12 — FUNERAL WISHES

12.1 I wish to be cremated and my ashes scattered in Sydney Harbour at the same location as Thomas's, if he has predeceased me.

12.2 If Thomas has not predeceased me, I wish my ashes to be scattered in the Rose Garden at Royal Botanic Garden Sydney.

---

## CLAUSE 13 — ON FIRST DEATH — EVERYTHING TO SURVIVOR

13.1 If my husband **THOMAS JAMES BANCROFT** shall survive me by twenty-eight (28) days, I give, devise and bequeath the whole of my estate, both real and personal, of whatever kind and wheresoever situate, to Thomas absolutely.

13.2 If Thomas shall not survive me by twenty-eight days, or if we die in circumstances rendering it uncertain which of us survived the other, then my estate shall be distributed in accordance with Clause 14 below as if Thomas had predeceased me.

---

## CLAUSE 14 — ON SECOND DEATH (OR IF THOMAS PREDECEASES ME)

If the provisions of Clause 13 do not take effect, or upon the subsequent death of Thomas (who shall be bound by the mutual will agreement to distribute in accordance with these terms), I direct that my estate shall be distributed in the same manner as set out in Clauses 7 and 8 of Part B above, mutatis mutandis.

---

## CLAUSE 15 — FAMILY PROVISION ACKNOWLEDGEMENT (SARAH)

15.1 The acknowledgement in Clause 9 of Part B applies equally to my estate. I confirm that I have given careful consideration to the needs of all three children — Jack, Emily, and Oliver — and believe the provision made in this mutual Will constitutes adequate and proper provision for each of them.

---

# PART D — SUPERANNUATION — NOT PART OF ESTATE

---

## CLAUSE 16 — SARAH'S SUPERANNUATION

16.1 I, Sarah, record that I hold a superannuation account with **AustralianSuper** (Member No. 12345678) with a current balance of approximately **AUD 850,000**.

16.2 I have made a binding death benefit nomination (BDBN) under the trust deed of AustralianSuper directing the trustee of the fund to pay the whole of my death benefit to my son **OLIVER JAMES WILLIAMS**. This nomination is current and was renewed on 1 January 2024.

16.3 My superannuation death benefit does not form part of my estate and is not governed by this Will. The BDBN is a direction to the trustee of the superannuation fund and takes effect outside my estate.

16.4 Thomas and I have agreed that the superannuation death benefit to Oliver, together with his share of the residuary estate, constitutes the provision we wish to make for Oliver, and this is reflected in the family provision acknowledgement in Clauses 9 and 15 above.

---

# PART E — GENERAL PROVISIONS

---

## CLAUSE 17 — SURVIVORSHIP

17.1 If any beneficiary named in either Part B or Part C of this Will shall die within twenty-eight (28) days of the death of the relevant Testator, or in circumstances rendering it uncertain which of them survived the other, then the gift to that beneficiary shall take effect as if that beneficiary had predeceased the relevant Testator.

---

## CLAUSE 18 — CONSTRUCTION

18.1 In this Will, words importing the singular include the plural and vice versa, and words importing any gender include all genders.

18.2 References to any statute shall include that statute as amended, re-enacted, or replaced from time to time.

18.3 This Will shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of New South Wales, Australia.

18.4 If any provision of this Will is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

18.5 The rule in *Saunders v Vautier* (1841) shall not apply to any trust created by this Will for so long as the relevant trust terms remain unfulfilled.
