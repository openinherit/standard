## What this will tests

This will exercises the following INHERIT capabilities:

- **`charitableRemainderDetails`** — Charitable Remainder Unitrust (CRUT) funded by film royalties, 5% annual payout, 20-year term for grandchildren's education
- **`generationSkipping`** — Generation-Skipping Transfer (GST) trust for ten grandchildren, using the GST exemption
- **Forced heirship (Philippine Civil Code)** — legitime entitlement of children to one-half of estate under Articles 886–914 of the Civil Code of the Philippines; Manila condo subject to Philippine succession law
- **Multi-jurisdiction** — California (community property rules, but testator is widowed so not directly applicable), Philippines (forced heirship), US federal (GST tax, estate tax)
- **Ongoing royalty income as asset** — film royalties from 12 films as a stream of income assigned to a trust
- **`governingJurisdictions`** — California default, Philippines for Manila property
- **`taxReliefEligibility`** — US federal estate tax marital deduction not applicable (widowed), charitable deduction for CRUT, GST exemption allocation
- **`asset-categories`** — real property (two jurisdictions), pension, brokerage, intellectual property (royalties)
- **`kinship`** — parent-child biological (three children), grandparent-grandchild (six grandchildren via three children)
- **`conditionType: "educational"`** — CRUT distributions conditioned on grandchildren being enrolled in accredited educational institutions
- **`administrationPhases`** — debts → funeral → specific bequests → trust funding → residue
- **`nonprobateTransfers`** — SAG-AFTRA pension (beneficiary designation outside estate)
- **`constructionClauses`** — California Probate Code construction rules, anti-lapse statute, no-contest clause
- **`connections`** — attorney relationship, SAG-AFTRA membership

**LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT OF MARIA SANTOS DE LA CRUZ**

I, **MARIA SANTOS DE LA CRUZ**, a citizen of the Republic of the Philippines and a lawful permanent resident of the United States of America, of legal age, being of sound and disposing mind and memory, and not acting under duress, menace, fraud, or undue influence of any person whatsoever, do hereby declare this instrument to be my Last Will and Testament, hereby revoking all prior Wills and Codicils heretofore made by me.

My Social Security Number ends in XXX-XX-4589.

My current address is 8821 Sunset Boulevard, West Hollywood, California 90069.

---

## ARTICLE I — DECLARATIONS

1.1 I am a widow. My late husband, **CARLOS SANTOS DE LA CRUZ SR.**, predeceased me on the 3rd day of March 2019.

1.2 I have three (3) living children, namely:

   (a) **ISABELLA SANTOS REYES**, born 14 May 1981, currently residing in Makati, Metro Manila, Philippines;

   (b) **CARLOS SANTOS DE LA CRUZ JR.**, born 22 September 1984, currently residing in Los Angeles, California; and

   (c) **GABRIELLA SANTOS PARK**, born 8 January 1988, currently residing in Los Angeles, California.

1.3 I have six (6) living grandchildren, namely: **SOFIA REYES** and **MARCO REYES** (children of Isabella); **DANIEL DE LA CRUZ** and **MARIA CLARA DE LA CRUZ** (children of Carlos Jr.); and **JAMES PARK** and **LILY PARK** (children of Gabriella).

1.4 I am a citizen of the Republic of the Philippines and maintain dual status as a lawful permanent resident of the United States. I am domiciled in the State of California.

---

## ARTICLE II — APPOINTMENT OF EXECUTOR

2.1 I appoint my son **CARLOS SANTOS DE LA CRUZ JR.** as Executor of this Will. If Carlos is unable or unwilling to serve, I appoint **RAYMOND CHAO**, Esq., of Chao & Associates, 1900 Avenue of the Stars, Suite 2700, Los Angeles, California 90067, as successor Executor.

2.2 My Executor shall serve without bond.

2.3 My Executor shall have all the powers granted to personal representatives under the California Probate Code, including but not limited to the Independent Administration of Estates Act (California Probate Code §§ 10400–10592), and shall have full power and authority to sell, lease, exchange, invest, reinvest, and manage all property of my estate, both real and personal, without court order.

2.4 In respect of my real property situated in the Republic of the Philippines (the Manila Condo described in Article V), I authorise my daughter **ISABELLA SANTOS REYES** to act as my special administratrix in the Philippines and to take all steps necessary under Philippine law to transfer, administer, and distribute the said property, including obtaining letters of administration from the appropriate Philippine court.

---

## ARTICLE III — PAYMENT OF DEBTS AND EXPENSES

3.1 I direct my Executor to pay all my legally enforceable debts, last illness expenses, funeral and burial expenses, costs of administration, and all estate, inheritance, and succession taxes (whether federal, state, or foreign) from my residuary estate, unless a specific source of payment is otherwise directed in this Will.

3.2 I direct that any estate tax, inheritance tax, or succession duty payable to the Republic of the Philippines in respect of the Manila Condo shall be paid from the value of that property and not from my California estate.

---

## ARTICLE IV — FUNERAL WISHES

4.1 I wish to be buried at Holy Cross Cemetery, 5835 West Slauson Avenue, Culver City, California 90230, next to my late husband Carlos Santos de la Cruz Sr.

4.2 I wish for a Catholic funeral Mass to be celebrated at the Church of the Good Shepherd, Beverly Hills, California, and for a separate memorial Mass to be said at San Agustin Church, Intramuros, Manila, for my family and friends in the Philippines.

4.3 These wishes are precatory and not binding on my Executor.

---

## ARTICLE V — SPECIFIC DEVISES AND BEQUESTS

### (a) Hollywood Hills Residence

5.1 I give, devise, and bequeath my real property situated at 2847 Mulholland Drive, Hollywood Hills, Los Angeles, California 90068 (the "Hollywood Hills Residence"), valued at approximately Five Million Dollars ($5,000,000), to my three children — **ISABELLA SANTOS REYES**, **CARLOS SANTOS DE LA CRUZ JR.**, and **GABRIELLA SANTOS PARK** — in equal shares, as tenants in common. If any child predeceases me leaving issue, such deceased child's share shall pass to his or her issue, per stirpes.

### (b) Manila Condominium — Subject to Philippine Law

5.2 I give, devise, and bequeath my condominium unit at Unit 42-B, The Residences at Greenbelt, Legaspi Street, Legaspi Village, Makati, Metro Manila, Philippines (the "Manila Condo"), to my three children in equal shares, subject to and in accordance with the succession laws of the Republic of the Philippines.

5.3 I acknowledge that under Articles 886 through 914 of the Civil Code of the Philippines, my children as compulsory heirs are collectively entitled to a legitime of one-half (1/2) of my net estate. I further acknowledge that this forced heirship provision applies mandatorily to the Manila Condo and to any other assets of mine that may be subject to Philippine jurisdiction.

5.4 I declare that it is my intention that the disposition of the Manila Condo herein shall satisfy the legitime requirements of Philippine law. In the event that the value of the Manila Condo (currently approximately Twenty-Five Million Philippine Pesos, PHP 25,000,000) is insufficient to satisfy the full legitime of all compulsory heirs, I direct my Executor to supplement the legitime from my residuary estate to the extent required by Philippine law.

5.5 I direct that the Manila Condo shall be administered and distributed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of the Philippines, and any dispute arising from its disposition shall be determined by the courts of the Philippines.

### (c) Brokerage Account — Personal Bequests

5.6 From the assets held in my Charles Schwab brokerage account (Account No. XXXX-7891), I make the following bequests:

   (a) To **ISABELLA SANTOS REYES** — Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000);

   (b) To **CARLOS SANTOS DE LA CRUZ JR.** — Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000); and

   (c) To **GABRIELLA SANTOS PARK** — Five Hundred Thousand Dollars ($500,000).

5.7 The balance remaining in the Charles Schwab brokerage account after the above bequests shall form part of the residuary estate.

## ARTICLE VI — CHARITABLE REMAINDER UNITRUST (CRUT) — GRANDCHILDREN'S EDUCATION

6.1 I hereby establish the **MARIA SANTOS DE LA CRUZ EDUCATION TRUST**, a Charitable Remainder Unitrust ("CRUT") within the meaning of Internal Revenue Code § 664(d)(2), to be funded from my film royalty income as set out below.

### Funding

6.2 I direct my Executor to transfer, assign, and convey to the Trustee of the CRUT all of my right, title, and interest in and to the film royalty income streams arising from my performances in the following twelve (12) motion pictures:

   (a) *Manila Sunset* (1978);
   (b) *The Pearl Divers* (1980);
   (c) *Zamboanga* (1982);
   (d) *Pacific Crossing* (1984);
   (e) *A Woman of Manila* (1986);
   (f) *The Consul's Wife* (1989);
   (g) *Return to Bataan* (1991);
   (h) *Golden Gate* (1993);
   (i) *The Last Hacienda* (1996);
   (j) *Mango Season* (1999);
   (k) *Daughter of the Sun* (2002); and
   (l) *Evening Star* (2005).

6.3 The said royalty income currently yields approximately Two Hundred Thousand Dollars ($200,000) per annum in aggregate.

### Terms of the CRUT

6.4 The CRUT shall have the following terms:

   (a) **Unitrust amount:** The Trustee shall pay to or among the Non-Charitable Beneficiaries (defined below) in each taxable year of the Trust a unitrust amount equal to five per centum (5%) of the net fair market value of the Trust assets, valued as of the first day of each taxable year of the Trust;

   (b) **Term:** The CRUT shall continue for a term of twenty (20) years from the date of my death, or until the Trust assets are exhausted, whichever is earlier;

   (c) **Non-Charitable Beneficiaries:** The unitrust payments shall be distributed equally among my six grandchildren — Sofia Reyes, Marco Reyes, Daniel de la Cruz, Maria Clara de la Cruz, James Park, and Lily Park — for the purpose of funding their post-secondary education at accredited colleges, universities, or vocational institutions;

   (d) **Educational condition:** A grandchild shall be eligible to receive his or her share of the unitrust amount only during such periods as he or she is enrolled as a full-time student at an accredited educational institution. If a grandchild is not enrolled, his or her share shall be accumulated within the Trust until he or she enrols, or shall be redistributed equally among the enrolled grandchildren, at the Trustee's discretion;

   (e) **Charitable remainder beneficiary:** Upon the expiration of the twenty-year term, the remaining assets in the CRUT shall be distributed to the **UNIVERSITY OF THE PHILIPPINES FOUNDATION, INC.** (or its successor), to establish a scholarship fund for students of performing arts; and

   (f) **Trustee:** The initial Trustee of the CRUT shall be **RAYMOND CHAO**, Esq., or his successor firm. The Trustee shall receive reasonable compensation as provided by California law.

6.5 The CRUT shall be administered in accordance with Internal Revenue Code § 664 and the regulations thereunder, and my Executor is authorised to take all steps necessary to obtain and maintain the Trust's tax-exempt status.

## ARTICLE VII — GENERATION-SKIPPING TRUST

7.1 I hereby establish the **SANTOS FAMILY GENERATION-SKIPPING TRUST** (the "GST Trust") and direct my Executor to allocate my remaining available Generation-Skipping Transfer tax exemption (currently $13,610,000 under the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017, as adjusted for inflation) to the GST Trust.

7.2 I direct my Executor to fund the GST Trust with the sum of **One Million Dollars ($1,000,000)** from my residuary estate.

7.3 The GST Trust shall be held for the benefit of my six grandchildren in equal shares. The Trustee may distribute income and principal to or among the grandchildren as the Trustee deems advisable for their health, education, maintenance, and support.

7.4 Upon each grandchild attaining the age of thirty (30) years, that grandchild's share of the GST Trust shall vest absolutely and shall be distributed to him or her outright and free of trust.

7.5 If any grandchild shall die before attaining the age of thirty years, and leaving issue surviving, the deceased grandchild's share shall be held in further trust for such issue upon the same terms. If any grandchild shall die before attaining the age of thirty years without leaving issue, the deceased grandchild's share shall be divided equally among the surviving grandchildren.

7.6 The Trustee of the GST Trust shall be **CARLOS SANTOS DE LA CRUZ JR.**, or if he is unable or unwilling to serve, **RAYMOND CHAO**, Esq.

## ARTICLE VIII — ASSETS PASSING OUTSIDE THIS WILL

8.1 I record that my Screen Actors Guild — American Federation of Television and Radio Artists (SAG-AFTRA) pension is the subject of a separate beneficiary designation and shall pass in accordance with that designation. The SAG-AFTRA pension does not form part of my probate estate and is not governed by this Will.

### (b) Other Beneficiary Designations

8.2 I record that any life insurance policies, retirement accounts, or other assets with designated beneficiaries shall pass in accordance with those designations and not under this Will.

## ARTICLE IX — RESIDUARY ESTATE

9.1 I give, devise, and bequeath all the rest, residue, and remainder of my estate, both real and personal, of whatever kind and wherever situated (excluding the Manila Condo, which is governed by Article V, Clauses 5.2–5.5), and after the payment of debts, expenses, taxes, specific bequests, and the funding of the CRUT and GST Trust, to my three children — **ISABELLA SANTOS REYES**, **CARLOS SANTOS DE LA CRUZ JR.**, and **GABRIELLA SANTOS PARK** — in equal shares.

9.2 If any child predeceases me leaving issue surviving, such child's share shall pass to his or her issue, per stirpes. If any child predeceases me without leaving issue, such child's share shall be divided equally between the surviving children.

---

## ARTICLE X — SURVIVORSHIP CLAUSE

10.1 If any beneficiary named in this Will, other than a charitable beneficiary, shall fail to survive me by thirty (30) days, then for all purposes of this Will, that beneficiary shall be deemed to have predeceased me.

---

## ARTICLE XI — NO-CONTEST CLAUSE

11.1 If any beneficiary under this Will shall, directly or indirectly, contest or attack this Will or any of its provisions, or seek to impair or invalidate any of its provisions, or conspire with or assist anyone attempting to do any of the foregoing, then the right of that person to take any interest given to him or her by this Will shall be determined as if he or she had predeceased me without leaving issue, to the fullest extent permitted by California Probate Code § 21311.

---

## ARTICLE XII — GENERAL PROVISIONS

12.1 This Will shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of California, except as specifically provided in Article V with respect to the Manila Condo.

12.2 If any provision of this Will is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the validity, legality, and enforceability of the remaining provisions shall not be affected or impaired thereby.

12.3 As used in this Will, the term "issue" means all lineal descendants of all degrees, and the term "per stirpes" shall have the meaning given to it by California Probate Code § 240.

12.4 References to the Internal Revenue Code shall mean the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended, and any successor provisions.
