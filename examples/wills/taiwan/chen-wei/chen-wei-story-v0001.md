## What this will tests

This will exercises the following INHERIT capabilities:

- **`taxReliefEligibility`** — Agricultural Property Relief (APR) under IHTA 1984 ss.115–124, conditional on active farming for 2 years post-death
- **`governingJurisdictions`** — England & Wales as default jurisdiction; Taiwan (Republic of China) for the Taipei apartment under Civil Code of the Republic of China
- **`conditionType: "employment"`** — Jia-Ying must actively farm the land for a minimum of 2 years to satisfy APR conditions
- **Cross-border assets** — freehold farm in Somerset and apartment in Taipei with different succession regimes
- **Custom conditions** — organic certification maintenance as an environmental obligation on the farm trust
- **Forced heirship (Taiwanese reserved share)** — Civil Code of the Republic of China Art. 1187 reserves a portion of the Taipei apartment for both children regardless of testamentary intent
- **Farm trust with purpose restrictions** — discretionary trust for agricultural land with environmental stewardship conditions
- **`conditionType: "restriction"`** — prohibition on sale of farm within 7 years (APR clawback period)
- **Multiple currency accounts** — GBP and TWD denominated assets in the same estate
- **`kinship`** — parent-child biological kinships, spousal relationship (marriage)
- **`asset-categories`** — agricultural land, residential property, vehicles, farm equipment, bank accounts
- **`administrationPhases`** — debts → funeral → legacies → trust establishment → residue
- **`constructionClauses`** — gender-neutral language, severability

**THIS IS THE LAST WILL AND TESTAMENT** of me, **CHEN WEI** (also known as **Wei Chen**), a national of the Republic of China (Taiwan) domiciled in England, of Willow Farm, Nether Stowey, Somerset TA5 1LQ (hereinafter called "the Testator").

---

## 1. REVOCATION

1.1 I hereby revoke all former Wills, Codicils and Testamentary Dispositions heretofore made by me, whether in England and Wales, the Republic of China (Taiwan), or any other jurisdiction, and declare this to be my last Will.

---

## 2. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

2.1 This Will shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales, save as expressly provided in Clause 2.2 below.

2.2 I direct that the disposition of my apartment at No. 42, Section 3, Xinyi Road, Da'an District, Taipei City 106, Republic of China (Taiwan) (the "Taipei Apartment") shall be governed by the laws of the Republic of China, including the provisions of the Civil Code relating to succession and reserved shares (teyiu fen). I acknowledge that Articles 1187 and 1223 of the Civil Code of the Republic of China provide that certain heirs are entitled to reserved shares (teyiu fen) which cannot be defeated by testamentary disposition, and I do not purport to override those provisions by this Will.

2.3 In the event of any conflict between the laws of England and Wales and the laws of the Republic of China (Taiwan) in relation to the Taipei Apartment, the laws of the Republic of China shall prevail.

---

## 3. APPOINTMENT OF EXECUTORS

3.1 I appoint **DAVID HUMPHREYS** of Humphreys & Blackwell Solicitors, 14 Castle Street, Bridgwater, Somerset TA6 3DE as the sole Executor and Trustee of this my Will in respect of all assets situated in England and Wales (hereinafter called "my English Executor").

3.2 In respect of the Taipei Apartment and any assets situated in the Republic of China (Taiwan), I direct that my son **CHEN JIA-WEI** (陳家瑋), currently residing at No. 88, Lane 12, Yongkang Street, Da'an District, Taipei City 106, Republic of China, shall act as executor and shall apply for the appropriate grant in Taiwan in accordance with the laws of the Republic of China.

3.3 If my English Executor shall predecease me or shall be unwilling or unable to act, I appoint my daughter **CHEN JIA-YING** (陳家盈) of Willow Farm, Nether Stowey, Somerset TA5 1LQ as substitute Executor and Trustee of my English estate.

3.4 My English Executor shall have all the powers conferred upon personal representatives and trustees by the Trustee Act 2000 and by law, including (without limitation) the power of sale, the power to appropriate, the power to insure, and the power to invest.

---

## 4. PAYMENT OF DEBTS, FUNERAL EXPENSES AND TESTAMENTARY EXPENSES

4.1 I direct my English Executor to pay all my just debts (save those relating exclusively to the Taipei Apartment), funeral expenses and testamentary expenses from my English estate.

4.2 I direct that any debts, taxes, levies or duties relating exclusively to the Taipei Apartment shall be discharged from the proceeds or income of that property, and not from my English estate.

---

## 5. FUNERAL WISHES

5.1 I wish to be cremated and my ashes scattered at Willow Farm, on the south-facing hillside overlooking the Quantock Hills, at a location known to my wife and daughter.

5.2 I wish for a memorial service to be held at St Mary's Church, Nether Stowey, and for a separate memorial gathering to be arranged by my son Jia-Wei in Taipei for the benefit of my family and friends in Taiwan.

5.3 These wishes are expressions of my desire and are not binding upon my Executors.

---

## 6. THE WILLOW FARM TRUST

### Establishment

6.1 I give, devise and bequeath all my freehold agricultural land and buildings comprising Willow Farm, Nether Stowey, Somerset TA5 1LQ, being approximately 200 acres of organic farmland registered at HM Land Registry under Title Number ST123456 (but excluding the farmhouse, which is dealt with separately in Clause 7 below), together with all farm buildings, barns, outbuildings, fixed equipment, irrigation systems, and all machinery and equipment used in connection with the farming of the said land (hereinafter together called "the Farm") to my English Executor upon trust as follows.

### Agricultural Property Relief — Conditions

6.2 I record that the Farm qualifies for Agricultural Property Relief ("APR") under sections 115 to 124 of the Inheritance Tax Act 1984, the land having been occupied by me for the purposes of agriculture throughout the period of seven years ending with the date of my death. I have farmed this land continuously since acquiring it in 2009.

6.3 I direct that, as a condition of receiving any beneficial interest in the Farm, my daughter **CHEN JIA-YING** (陳家盈) shall:

   (a) reside at or in the immediate vicinity of Willow Farm;

   (b) be actively and personally engaged in the farming of the land on a full-time basis; and

   (c) continue to satisfy paragraphs (a) and (b) for a minimum continuous period of two (2) years from the date of my death,

(hereinafter the "Farming Condition"), so as to preserve the eligibility of the Farm for Agricultural Property Relief and to prevent any clawback of the relief by His Majesty's Revenue and Customs.

6.4 If the Farming Condition is satisfied, the Farm shall vest absolutely in **CHEN JIA-YING** upon the expiry of the said two-year period, free from any trust, but subject to the environmental obligations set out in Clause 6.6 below.

### Restriction on Sale

6.5 I direct that the Farm shall not be sold, transferred, mortgaged, charged, or otherwise disposed of (whether in whole or in part) within a period of seven (7) years from the date of my death, save with the prior written consent of my English Executor (or his successor) and only if such disposal would not give rise to a clawback of Agricultural Property Relief. For the avoidance of doubt, this restriction applies to Jia-Ying personally and to any successor in title during the said seven-year period.

### Environmental Obligations

6.6 I direct that, for so long as my daughter Jia-Ying or any successor in title holds the Farm, the following environmental obligations shall attach to the land and shall be conditions of the beneficial interest:

   (a) The Farm shall maintain its organic certification with the Soil Association (or such other recognised organic certification body as may from time to time be the appropriate certifying authority in England) and shall not revert to conventional farming methods;

   (b) No part of the Farm shall be converted to non-agricultural use (including but not limited to residential development, solar farm installation, or mineral extraction) without the prior written consent of a qualified environmental assessment and the approval of the trustees of this Will;

   (c) The existing hedgerows, woodland copses and wildlife corridors on the Farm shall be maintained and, where reasonably practicable, enhanced; and

   (d) The watercourse running through the Farm (the Stowey Brook tributary) shall not be polluted, diverted, or obstructed.

6.7 I acknowledge that these environmental obligations are moral and testamentary in nature and may not be enforceable as covenants running with the land. I express the strongest possible wish that Jia-Ying and any successor in title shall honour them.

### Failure of the Farming Condition

6.8 If the Farming Condition set out in Clause 6.3 is not satisfied (whether because Jia-Ying predeceases me, is unwilling to farm, or ceases to satisfy the condition before the expiry of the two-year period), then the Farm shall be held by my English Executor upon trust for sale, and the net proceeds of sale (after payment of all costs, taxes and any clawback of APR) shall be divided equally between my two children, **CHEN JIA-YING** and **CHEN JIA-WEI**, in equal shares absolutely.

---

## 7. FARMHOUSE — LIFE INTEREST FOR MY WIFE

7.1 I give, devise and bequeath the farmhouse known as Willow Farmhouse, Nether Stowey, Somerset TA5 1LQ (being the principal dwelling house and its curtilage, but excluding the agricultural land and farm buildings dealt with in Clause 6 above) to my English Executor upon trust to permit my wife **LIN MEI-HUA** (林美華) to reside therein for the remainder of her natural life, rent-free, and to receive any income derived therefrom (hereinafter the "Life Interest").

7.2 During the subsistence of the Life Interest, my wife shall be responsible for the day-to-day maintenance and upkeep of the farmhouse, the payment of council tax, utilities, and insurance premiums, and shall keep the property in reasonable repair.

7.3 Upon the death of my wife, or upon her voluntarily surrendering the Life Interest in writing, the farmhouse shall pass absolutely to my daughter **CHEN JIA-YING**.

7.4 If Jia-Ying shall predecease my wife or shall have predeceased me, the farmhouse shall upon the termination of the Life Interest pass absolutely to my son **CHEN JIA-WEI**.

---

## 8. TAIPEI APARTMENT — SUBJECT TO TAIWANESE LAW

8.1 I give, devise and bequeath my apartment at No. 42, Section 3, Xinyi Road, Da'an District, Taipei City 106, Republic of China (Taiwan), Taipei Land Office Registration No. TP-2004-087654 (the "Taipei Apartment"), to my two children **CHEN JIA-YING** and **CHEN JIA-WEI** in equal shares absolutely, subject to the provisions of the Civil Code of the Republic of China and, in particular, to the reserved shares (teyiu fen) to which they are each entitled under Articles 1187 and 1223 thereof.

8.2 I record that under the Civil Code of the Republic of China, Article 1223, the reserved share (teyiu fen) of a lineal descendant is one-half (1/2) of that descendant's intestate share. As I have two children who are lineal descendants, their combined reserved share is one-half of the estate. I do not purport to diminish these reserved shares by this Will.

8.3 I acknowledge that my wife Lin Mei-Hua is also entitled to a reserved share under Taiwanese law as a surviving spouse. I direct that the Taipei Apartment shall be administered and distributed in accordance with Taiwanese succession law, and any dispute arising from the disposition of this property shall be determined by the courts of the Republic of China.

8.4 I direct my son Chen Jia-Wei, as executor for the Taiwanese estate, to discharge all local taxes, duties and expenses relating to the Taipei Apartment from the proceeds or income of that property.

---

## 9. BANK ACCOUNTS AND FINANCIAL ASSETS

9.1 I give and bequeath the balance standing to the credit of my HSBC UK current account (account number 12345678, sort code 40-20-15) and my HSBC UK savings account (account number 87654321, sort code 40-20-15) to my wife **LIN MEI-HUA** absolutely.

9.2 I give and bequeath the balance standing to the credit of my HSBC Taiwan New Taiwan Dollar savings account (account number TW-9876543210, Taipei Xinyi Branch) to my son **CHEN JIA-WEI** absolutely, subject to the laws of the Republic of China governing the transfer of such funds.

9.3 I give and bequeath the balance standing to the credit of my HSBC UK ISA (account number 11223344, sort code 40-20-15) to my daughter **CHEN JIA-YING** absolutely.

---

## 10. FARM EQUIPMENT

10.1 I give and bequeath all farm machinery, equipment, tools, vehicles used exclusively for agricultural purposes (including but not limited to the John Deere 6120M tractor, registration number WV19 FRM, and the Massey Ferguson 4709 tractor, registration number WV21 AGR), livestock handling equipment, and all other chattels used in connection with the farming of Willow Farm, to my daughter **CHEN JIA-YING** absolutely, conditional upon the Farming Condition in Clause 6.3 being satisfied.

10.2 If the Farming Condition is not satisfied, the said farm equipment shall be sold by my English Executor and the net proceeds shall form part of the residuary estate.

---

## 11. RESIDUARY ESTATE

11.1 Subject to the payment of my debts, funeral expenses and testamentary expenses, and subject to the specific devises, bequests and trusts set out above, I give, devise and bequeath all the rest and residue of my estate, of whatsoever kind and wheresoever situate (including any property over which I have a general power of appointment) (but excluding the Taipei Apartment, which is dealt with in Clause 8 above), to my wife **LIN MEI-HUA** and my two children **CHEN JIA-YING** and **CHEN JIA-WEI** in the following shares:

   (a) To my wife Lin Mei-Hua — fifty per centum (50%);

   (b) To my daughter Chen Jia-Ying — twenty-five per centum (25%); and

   (c) To my son Chen Jia-Wei — twenty-five per centum (25%).

11.2 If any of the above-named beneficiaries shall predecease me, his or her share of the residuary estate shall be divided equally among the surviving beneficiaries named in this Clause.

---

## 12. SURVIVORSHIP CLAUSE

12.1 If any beneficiary named in this Will shall die within twenty-eight (28) days of the date of my death, or in circumstances rendering it uncertain which of us survived the other, then the gift to that beneficiary shall take effect as if that beneficiary had predeceased me.

---

## 13. GENERAL PROVISIONS

13.1 The Apportionment Act 1870 shall not apply to my estate.

13.2 Words importing the singular shall include the plural and vice versa, and words importing any gender shall include every gender.

13.3 References to any statute or statutory provision shall include that statute or provision as from time to time amended, extended, re-enacted or consolidated.

13.4 If any provision of this Will shall be held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

13.5 This Will (save for the provisions relating to the Taipei Apartment in Clause 8) shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.
